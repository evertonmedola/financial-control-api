## Phase Goal

**As a** signed-in user, **I want to** record income/expense transactions linked to an account and a category, and transfer money between two of my own accounts as a single atomic operation, **so that** my account balances always reflect reality precisely (no floating-point drift) and transfers never get miscounted as spending once budgets and reports exist.

The "so that" clause above is a direct, non-invented restructuring sourced from 04-CONTEXT.md's `<domain>` Phase Boundary note ("Transfers must never appear in category spend totals... Concurrency correctness... is this phase's single highest-risk piece of business logic") — no new facts added, mirroring 03-01-PLAN.md's identical handling of this same situation. If a fuller SPIDR-split user story is wanted, run `/gsd mvp-phase 04` and replant.)*
